Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 27, 2026 · Contact: aripuspondaru@gmail.com

Summary: PomoNow works locally in your browser without an account. If you choose to sign in for Cloud Sync, we only store your account email, optional display name, and your workspace data. We never sell your data or read your private Google files.

1. Guest Mode (Local-Only Storage)

When you use PomoNow without signing in, your tasks, timer preferences, Sound Studio mixes, themes, and focus analytics stay solely inside your browser’s localStorage and are not transmitted to our database.

2. Data Collected When You Sign In

  • Account Information: Your email address, optional display name, optional profile picture URL, and subscription plan status.
  • Synced Workspace Data: Your tasks, subtasks, session logs, daily focus stats, and theme/timer settings stored so you can sync across devices.

3. Google Sign-In Disclosure

If you choose Continue with Google, PomoNow requests only basic identity scopes (email, name, and profile picture) strictly to authenticate your account. We do not request access to Gmail, Google Drive, Google Calendar, or contacts, and we never sell or share your Google user data with advertisers or data brokers.

4. Infrastructure & Payment Providers

To deliver PomoNow securely, we rely on trusted infrastructure providers:

  • Vercel: Static web hosting and serverless API execution.
  • Supabase: User authentication and PostgreSQL cloud database with Row-Level Security so only your authenticated session can access your workspace.
  • Payment Processors (When Enabled): Subscription checkouts and card/QRIS handling are processed by third-party payment providers. PomoNow never stores full credit card numbers on its servers.

5. Your Rights: Export & Deletion

  • Export Anytime: Export your focus session logs and timesheets anytime as CSV or JSON in Analytics.
  • Delete Your Account: Email aripuspondaru@gmail.com from your account email address to request permanent deletion of your profile and synced workspace data. Deletion requests are completed within 7 days.
