While PomoNow is in Soft Beta, no subscription fees are charged and Pro features are unlocked free of charge.

Once paid Pro subscriptions are active, you are eligible for a full 100% refund if requested within 14 calendar days of your initial subscription purchase or an unintended renewal charge.

You can cancel your subscription anytime before your next billing date. After canceling, your Pro access and Cloud Sync remain active until the end of your current paid billing period, and you will not be billed again.

Send an email to aripuspondaru@gmail.com with:

Approved refunds are issued within 3–5 business days back to your original payment method (depending on your bank or payment provider’s settlement speed).
