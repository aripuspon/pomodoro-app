Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective Date: November 27, 2026 · Support: aripuspondaru@gmail.com

3-Day Free Trial (No Card Required): Every new account includes a 3-day free trial of PomoNow Pro before you ever pay. Because you can try all Pro features first, all subscription payments are final and non-refundable once billed.

1. 3-Day Free Trial (No Card Required)

When you create an account on PomoNow, you receive a 3-day free trial of Pro features and Cloud Sync with no credit card required. At the end of Day 3, your account smoothly reverts to the Free plan with zero surprise charges unless you choose to subscribe.

2. No Refunds After Purchase

Because every user can evaluate all Pro features during the 3-day free trial prior to subscribing, all Pro subscription payments are final and non-refundable once billed.

Exceptions apply only for verified duplicate or erroneous billing charges, unresolved technical failures preventing Pro activation on your account, or where mandatory local consumer law requires otherwise.

3. Cancel Anytime

You can cancel your subscription anytime before your next billing date. After canceling, your Pro access and Cloud Sync remain active until the end of your current paid billing period, and you will not be billed again.

4. Soft Beta & Billing Support

While PomoNow is in Soft Beta, no subscription fees are charged. If you believe a duplicate or billing error occurred after paid plans launch, email aripuspondaru@gmail.com with your account email and receipt ID for review within 3–5 business days.
