Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective Date: November 27, 2026 · Support: aripuspondaru@gmail.com

Tiered Refund Policy: Request a refund anytime within 14 days of purchase — 100% in the first 3 days, 70% within Week 1 (Days 4–7), and 50% within Week 2 (Days 8–14).

1. Soft Beta Status

While PomoNow is in Soft Beta, no subscription fees are charged and Pro features are unlocked free of charge.

2. Tiered Refund Schedule

Once paid Pro subscriptions are active, refund eligibility is based on the number of calendar days since your initial purchase or renewal charge:

  • First 3 days (Days 1–3): 100% full refund
  • Within Week 1 (Days 4–7): 70% refund
  • Within Week 2 (Days 8–14): 50% refund
  • After Day 14: Non-refundable for the active period (you can still cancel anytime to stop future renewals).

Where local consumer protection law mandates a different minimum refund right, we honor the applicable statutory requirement.

3. Cancel Anytime

You can cancel your subscription anytime before your next billing date. After canceling, your Pro access and Cloud Sync remain active until the end of your current paid billing period, and you will not be billed again.

4. How to Request a Refund

Send an email to aripuspondaru@gmail.com with:

  • The email address associated with your PomoNow account
  • Your order or receipt ID (if available)

Approved refunds are issued within 3–5 business days back to your original payment method (depending on your bank or payment provider’s settlement speed).
